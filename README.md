One image summing up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026: 54 verified stories across 6 continents — Cyclone Akvan's actual landfall at Gopalpur (70–80 km/h, no deaths reported, 370,000 evacuated), Russia's overnight missile-and-drone barrage on Kyiv that damaged three Dnipro bridges and violated Polish airspace, Myanmar calling the ICJ's Rohingya genocide ruling unfair, the Shenzhen trilateral's four-point peace declaration, GTA VI's estimated $2.36bn launch day, Anthropic closing 17% up on its record IPO while Asian markets fell, a youth strike across 25+ European nations, and Mongolia's president resigning — with a coordinate-drawn world map, a UTC timeline strip, a date-check box, and a source list. Drawn in code with Python's standard library only; the program writes entry.png deterministically, with no network and no photos.

Date handling: the big 19 November stories (ICJ verdict, Shenzhen summit, Berlin summit, Riyadh tower strike, GTA VI figures, Voyager 1, Anthropic debut) are dated "(19 NOV)" on their cards, and the date-check box lists only items from 20 November. Claims are labelled as claims, and Sensor Tower's GTA VI figures are third-party estimates, not official Take-Two numbers. The satirical "merged nuclear arsenals" story circulating online is not in the image — the real Shenzhen outcome is the four-point Declaration Toward Peace.
